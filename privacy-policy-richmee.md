**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the Richmee app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Bruce Liang (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad Supported service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

This policy explains what personal information the Application collects, why it is collected, how it is collected, how long it is kept, and the rights you have over it.

Last updated: 2026-08-24

**What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?**

Registration is not required to use the Application, and the Service Provider does not ask you for your name, phone number, email address or bank account. Your expense records, budgets and asset data are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to the Service Provider's servers.

However, in order to display advertisements, keep the Application stable and prevent fraud, the Application and the third-party SDKs embedded in it automatically collect a limited set of device information, including personal information as defined by applicable law. The full list is set out below.

*Personal information and device information collected*

- **Device serial number (SN)** — Collected automatically by the embedded advertising and analytics SDKs when the Application starts. Purpose: to identify the device uniquely so that advertising impressions and clicks can be de-duplicated, invalid or fraudulent traffic can be detected, and advertising revenue can be settled. Scope: the SN value only; it is not combined with your financial records.
- **ANDROID ID** — Collected automatically by the embedded advertising and analytics SDKs when the Application starts. Purpose: to identify the device for advertisement delivery and frequency capping, anti-fraud and risk control, statistical analysis of active devices and retention, and crash diagnosis. Scope: the ANDROID ID value only; it is not combined with your financial records.
- **Device and system information** (device model, brand, operating system version, screen resolution, system language, time zone, network connection type and carrier information) — Collected automatically when the Application starts or when an advertisement is requested. Purpose: to adapt the interface to your device, deliver advertisements that can be rendered correctly on your device, and diagnose faults.
- **IP address and approximate (city/region level) location derived from it** — Collected automatically when the Application communicates with advertising or analytics services. Purpose: network communication, selecting the advertising content and language appropriate for your region, and security and anti-fraud checks.
- **Application operation and crash logs** (screens opened, features used, error messages, crash stack traces, timestamps) — Collected automatically while you use the Application. Purpose: to fix defects, improve stability and understand which features are used.
- **Advertising interaction records** (advertisement identifiers, impressions, clicks) — Collected automatically when an advertisement is shown or clicked. Purpose: advertising delivery, measurement, anti-fraud and revenue settlement.

The Service Provider does not use the above information to identify you personally, does not sell it, and does not use it for any purpose other than those stated above.

*How the information is collected*

All of the information listed above is collected automatically through the software development kits (SDKs) integrated in the Application; you are not asked to type it in. Collection begins only after you have started the Application and, where required by law, after you have agreed to this Privacy Policy. The Application does not collect this information in the background after it has been closed.

*Legal basis and consent*

The collection described above is carried out on the basis of your consent, which you give by accepting this Privacy Policy when you first launch the Application, and, where applicable, on the basis of the Service Provider's legitimate interest in securing the service and preventing advertising fraud.

**Does the Application require any permissions?**

The Application requests the following permissions. You may refuse or later withdraw any of them in your device settings; the core expense-tracking features will continue to work.

- **Network access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)** — required to load advertisements, report crash information and check network availability.
- **Storage / photo selection** — used only when you choose to attach a picture to a record or to export or back up your data. Files are read or written only after you select them, and pictures are not uploaded to the Service Provider's servers.
- **Notifications** — used only to remind you about budgets and bookkeeping if you enable reminders.

The Application does not request access to your contacts, call logs, SMS messages, microphone, or precise location.

**Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?**

No. The Application does not collect GPS or other precise location information. Only the approximate region derived from your IP address may be used, as described above.

**Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?**

Yes. Because the Application is supported by advertising, the device identifiers and other information listed above are collected and processed by the following third-party service providers through their SDKs. Each of them processes the data under its own privacy policy:

- **Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google LLC)** — advertisement delivery, measurement and anti-fraud. Data received: device identifiers (including the ANDROID ID and the device serial number), IP address, device and system information, advertising interaction records. Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Firebase Crashlytics / Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC)** — crash reporting and usage statistics. Data received: device identifiers (including the ANDROID ID and the device serial number), IP address, device and system information, crash and operation logs. Privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

These are the only third-party SDKs embedded in the Application. The Service Provider does not share the information listed above with any other party.

The Service Provider may also disclose information where this is required by law, by a court order or by a competent authority, or where it is necessary to investigate fraud or protect the rights and safety of users.

Because these providers may operate servers outside your country or region, the information described above may be transferred across borders and stored on their servers. Such transfers are carried out in accordance with the safeguards described in the privacy policies linked above.

**How long is the information kept?**

The Service Provider does not maintain its own server-side copy of your records; your bookkeeping data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Application. Device identifiers, logs and advertising interaction records are retained by the third-party providers listed above for the periods stated in their own privacy policies, and only for as long as is necessary for advertising settlement, anti-fraud and statistical purposes.
