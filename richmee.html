Richmee

Smart & Simple Finance

Richmee is a modern expense tracker that helps you manage your money effortlessly. Track spending, organize assets, and gain insights to make smarter financial decisions every day. Download App

Richmee Project

📊 Insightful Charts

View your expenses and income through interactive charts and graphs. Quickly spot where your money is going, identify spending patterns, and make informed decisions with visual insights.

💰 Comprehensive Asset Management

Keep track of all your financial accounts, including cash, bank balances, and digital wallets. Monitor your total assets, net worth, and account balances in real-time for a complete financial overview.

⏰ Scheduled & Template Recording

Never miss a recurring payment or expense. Set up scheduled entries for bills or subscriptions, and use customizable templates to log similar transactions instantly, saving time and avoiding errors.

📈 Monthly Comparisons

Compare your spending and income month by month with detailed breakdowns. Identify trends, track progress toward your budgeting goals, and get actionable insights to improve your financial habits.
